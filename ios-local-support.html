Serve It Up — iOS local-save edition support

This page applies to the local-save edition prepared for version 1.0. For editions with iCloud controls, see the existing support page.

Contact support

Email games@nostalgicgamestudios.com with your device model, app version and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Restore an expansion purchase

Open an expansion shop's unlock prompt and select Restore purchase. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restore purchase restores paid access, not your game progress. Purchases do not transfer from Google Play.

Game progress

This edition saves progress on your device and has no cloud-sync control. Deleting the app can remove progress. Apple device backups may contain app data depending on your backup settings. Normal and Hard mode progress are separate.

Offline play

The game can be played offline. Buying or restoring an expansion needs a connection to the App Store. If checkout is pending, wait for Apple's approval or payment confirmation.
