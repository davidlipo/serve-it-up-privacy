Serve It Up

One little shop. A whole street of possibilities.

Build a bustling neighbourhood, one delicious order at a time. Start in your bagel shop, then expand into pizza, sushi, pasta and ice cream. Walk between stations, assemble orders, serve guests and upgrade your equipment.

Choose Normal mode for relaxed stock management or Hard mode for limited supplies and street events. Optional Google Play Games cloud saves let you back up progress. A single optional purchase unlocks access to the final three shops; earn in-game coins to buy and improve them.

Android release currently in testing.
