Serve It Up Support

For help with gameplay, purchases, or saved progress, email games@nostalgicgamestudios.com. Include your device model, app version, and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Restore your iPhone purchase

Open an expansion shop’s unlock prompt and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoring the expansion does not restore saved progress.

Saved progress

Progress is saved on your device. On iPhone, open Settings → Edit cloud save to manage iCloud syncing. Android uses Google Play Games. Saves and purchases do not transfer between Apple and Android.
