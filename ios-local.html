Serve It Up Privacy Policy — iOS

Local-save edition · Version 1.0 · Updated 2 October 2026

This notice describes the iOS version of Serve It Up by Nostalgic Game Studios.

Game progress on your device

The game stores progress, coins, shop names, upgrades, character choices, settings and a limited graphics diagnostic history on your device. Normal and Hard progress are saved separately. No studio account is required. This version does not offer cloud-save synchronization. Device backups may include game data depending on your Apple backup settings.

Purchases

Optional expansion purchases and restoration are handled by Apple's App Store. The game uses Apple's StoreKit service to check whether you own the expansion. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple processes transactions under its own privacy policy. Purchases do not transfer from Google Play.

Analytics and advertising

This iOS version does not send gameplay analytics to the studio and does not include advertising or cross-app tracking. Graphics diagnostics remain on your device unless you choose to share a report with support.

Support and external links

If you email games@nostalgicgamestudios.com, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. Do not send payment-card details. Opening the online privacy policy visits our website in your browser, where the hosting provider processes connection information needed to deliver the page.

Removing data

Deleting the app removes its local game data. Backups can be managed in your Apple device or account settings. Removing local game data does not cancel a completed purchase; Apple manages purchase history and refund requests. Contact games@nostalgicgamestudios.com for questions about information you have sent to support.
