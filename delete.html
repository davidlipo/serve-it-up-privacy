Delete Serve It Up data

Nostalgic Game Studios · Serve It Up

Delete device progress

In Android Settings, open Apps → Serve It Up → Storage, then clear the app's data. This removes local progress, settings and the local backup kept before a cloud restore. It resets both Normal and Hard mode. Manage Android device backups separately in your device or Google account settings.

Delete Google Play cloud progress

First open Cloud save in Serve It Up and turn off automatic syncing on each device. Then open your Google Play Games profile and delete the game data for Serve It Up. Follow Google's game-data deletion instructions. This removes the cloud game data controlled by Play Games; it does not clear your local device saves. Do not upload a local save again if you want the cloud copy to remain deleted.

Request deletion of support information

Email games@nostalgicgamestudios.com with the subject “Serve It Up data deletion”. Use the email address you previously contacted us from, and identify the support conversation you want deleted. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents.

What may remain

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle your request, related follow-up, or applicable legal obligations. Google controls purchase records and service diagnostics under its policies. Deleting game data does not cancel the shop expansion purchase or erase transaction records that Google retains for legal or financial purposes. Device backups are controlled separately by your device settings.

See the Serve It Up privacy policy for details.
