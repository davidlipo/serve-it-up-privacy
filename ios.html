Serve It Up Privacy Policy — iOS

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Nostalgic Game Studios provides Serve It Up. This policy describes the iOS edition. The Android and web editions have different services and policies.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, coins, shop names, upgrades, character choices, settings and limited graphics diagnostic history on your device. Normal and Hard progress are separate. No studio account is required. When iCloud is available, the game synchronizes both difficulty modes and sound settings to your private iCloud storage using Apple CloudKit. This includes coins, shop names, upgrades and character choices. An app-specific iCloud account identifier is used locally to detect account changes. Conflicting progress requires you to choose which save to keep; a local backup is kept before restoring. You can turn off automatic syncing in the cloud-save screen and keep playing offline. This edition does not connect to Google Play Games. Device backups may also include game data according to your Apple device and account settings.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time expansion purchase. The game uses Apple's StoreKit service to check verified purchase entitlements and restore access. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Purchase and account information handled by Apple is subject to Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Restore purchase restores access to paid shops; it does not restore game progress. Android purchases do not transfer to the iOS edition.

Advertising and analytics

This iOS edition includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Saved-game contents are stored in your private iCloud database when syncing is enabled; the app does not publish them or send them to a studio analytics service. Apple processes this data to provide iCloud. The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or location and does not use an advertising identifier. Apple may process App Store and diagnostic information according to its services and your device settings.

Support

Graphics diagnostics stay on your device unless you choose to share a report with support. Opening the online policy visits our website; the hosting provider processes connection information needed to deliver the page.

If you email games@nostalgicgamestudios.com, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details. Email providers process correspondence to deliver and store it. Support correspondence is retained as needed for your request, follow-up and applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of information sent to support.

Retention and choices

Deleting the app can remove local progress. Turning off sync or deleting the app does not delete your existing iCloud save; manage the app’s iCloud data in Apple’s storage settings. Offloading the app may retain its data. Backups may restore progress later; manage backups separately in your device or Apple account settings. Deleting game data does not cancel purchases or erase Apple's transaction records. Locally stored data is protected by your device's security settings; no storage system is guaranteed completely secure.

Children

The game has no in-game chat, public profiles or advertising. Parents and guardians can manage purchases using Apple's family and purchase-approval settings. Contact us if a child has sent personal information to support so we can address the request.

Policy changes and contact

We will update this policy if this edition's data practices change. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information or raise a concern with a data-protection authority.
