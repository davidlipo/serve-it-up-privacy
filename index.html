Serve It Up Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

Nostalgic Game Studios provides Serve It Up. This policy describes the current Android game distributed through Google Play. It does not describe a separately hosted web edition.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, coins, shop names, upgrades, character choices and settings on your device so that you can continue playing. Normal and Hard mode progress are stored separately. No game account is required, and the current Android version does not upload gameplay progress to our servers or provide Google Play Games cloud saves.

Android's backup service may back up app data depending on your device and account settings. You can manage those backups through your Android and Google account settings.

Purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time shop expansion purchase. The game exchanges purchase information with Google Play to check ownership, acknowledge a purchase and restore access. It stores a signed purchase receipt on your device. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Google processes purchase and account information under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Restoring a purchase restores access to the expansion; it does not restore gameplay progress.

Advertising, analytics and device access

The current Android game contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location, and does not use the Android advertising identifier. We do not sell personal information or use gameplay information for targeted advertising.

Contacting support

If you email games@nostalgicgamestudios.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate problems. Please do not include passwords or payment-card details. Email providers process these messages to deliver and store correspondence.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on your device until removed. You can delete local app data using Android's app storage settings; this resets your progress. Uninstalling may also remove local data, although device backups may restore it later. Manage or delete any backups separately in your device or account settings.

Purchase records held by Google are managed under Google's policies. Deleting local game data does not cancel a purchase or erase Google's transaction records. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request, related follow-up, or applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information you have sent to support.

Children

The game can be played without providing personal information to Nostalgic Game Studios. It has no in-game chat, public profiles or advertising. Parents and guardians can manage purchases through Google Play's family and purchase-approval settings. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so that we can address the request and delete that information where appropriate.

Security and your choices

We limit the information used by the game to its functionality. Your device's security and backup settings help protect locally stored progress. No storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to raise a concern with a data-protection authority. Contact us with privacy requests.

Policy changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date. If features such as account-based cloud saves are introduced, we will explain their data handling before or when they become available.
