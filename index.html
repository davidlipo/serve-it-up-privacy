Serve It Up Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Nostalgic Game Studios provides Serve It Up. This policy describes the current Android game distributed through Google Play. It does not describe a separately hosted web edition.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, coins, shop names, upgrades, character choices and settings on your device so that you can continue playing. Normal and Hard mode progress are stored separately. No separate studio account is required. You can play using device saves without enabling cloud saves. Earlier Android versions without a Cloud save button store progress on the device only.

Android's backup service may back up app data depending on your device and account settings. You can manage those backups through your Android and Google account settings.

Google Play Games and optional cloud saves

Android versions use Google Play Games Services. The game attempts to connect when it starts, and Google may ask you to sign in or create a Play Games profile. You can dismiss this and play offline. Cloud syncing is enabled by default and can be turned off in Edit cloud save. Empty cloud slots are backed up automatically; differing device and cloud progress requires your choice before either version is replaced. Normal and Hard progress are saved together. Saved information includes earned coins, shop names, upgrades, character choices, tutorials and settings. We do not include payment-card information or purchase receipts in cloud saves.

Google stores cloud progress linked to your Play Games profile. The game uses your game-specific player ID to keep syncing linked to the selected profile and prevent accidental syncing after an account change. Google Play Games Services also processes gamer identity, SDK usage analytics and diagnostic information for its service and reliability. We do not request your friends list or add an advertising service. Optional game analytics is described below. Google encrypts Play Games data in transit. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can turn off automatic syncing from Edit cloud save. This leaves existing cloud data in place. To remove that data, first turn off syncing, then delete Serve It Up's game data through your Google Play Games profile. Google's instructions are at https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646. Clearing device data alone does not delete cloud saves, and cloud deletion does not cancel purchases. Cloud data remains until deleted through those controls or under Google's retention policies. A local backup is retained before cloud restoration and is removed when you clear the app's data.

Purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time shop expansion purchase. The game exchanges purchase information with Google Play to check ownership, acknowledge a purchase and restore access. It stores a signed purchase receipt on your device. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Google processes purchase and account information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Restoring a purchase restores access to the expansion; it does not restore gameplay progress.

Advertising, analytics and device access

The Android game contains no advertising SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location, and does not use the Android advertising identifier. We do not sell personal information or use gameplay information for targeted advertising.

In versions offering Play statistics, players aged 18 or over can choose to share optional statistics with Nostalgic Game Studios through Google Analytics for Firebase. It is off before you choose, and unavailable to players who select Under 18. Selecting Prefer not to say keeps it off. Your age-group choice stays on your device; we do not ask for your date of birth. This setting is separate from cloud saves and is not included in their backups.

If you opt in, we collect app launches and sessions, tutorials, shop and difficulty selections, shift results, coin totals, upgrades, progression milestones, purchase and restoration outcomes, loading times and general save/error results. Firebase also processes an app-instance identifier, device/app information and approximate location derived from masked IP addresses, and may automatically record in-app purchase product and price information. The app-instance identifier connects sessions from the same installation; it is not linked by us to your Google Play Games player ID. We do not send custom shop names, character choices, email addresses, purchase receipts, saved-game contents or raw error messages to this analytics service.

We use these statistics to understand returning players, improve balance and tutorials, and diagnose reliability problems. Google processes analytics on our behalf and may process data outside your country; see https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not enable advertising identifiers, Google signals, advertising personalisation or advertising-account integrations for this feature. Gameplay never requires an analytics connection; opted-in events may be queued on the device while offline and sent after reconnection.

You can stop sharing in Settings → Play statistics. This disables future collection and resets the local analytics identifier; it does not erase previously collected reports. Clearing app data also clears your local choice and returns statistics to off. We retain analytics according to the retention controls configured for our Google Analytics property, and aggregated reports may remain longer. For an analytics access or deletion request, use Settings → Play statistics → Get privacy request reference before turning statistics off, then send the reference to games@nostalgicgamestudios.com. We use it only to locate the relevant analytics records and handle the request. Deletion of linked records does not necessarily remove previously aggregated reports. Because we do not link analytics to your name or email, we may not be able to identify past records from an email address alone.

Contacting support

If you email games@nostalgicgamestudios.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate problems. Please do not include passwords or payment-card details. Email providers process these messages to deliver and store correspondence.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on your device until removed. You can delete local app data using Android's app storage settings; this resets your progress. Uninstalling may also remove local data, although device backups may restore it later. Manage or delete any backups separately in your device or account settings.

Purchase records held by Google are managed under Google's policies. Deleting local game data does not cancel a purchase or erase Google's transaction records. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request, related follow-up, or applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information you have sent to support.

Children

The game can be played without providing personal information to Nostalgic Game Studios. It has no in-game chat, public profiles or advertising. Optional Play statistics are not enabled for players who identify themselves as under 18, or who do not provide an age group. Parents and guardians can manage purchases through Google Play's family and purchase-approval settings. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so that we can address the request and delete that information where appropriate.

Security and your choices

We limit the information used by the game to its functionality. Your device's security and backup settings help protect locally stored progress. No storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to raise a concern with a data-protection authority. Contact us with privacy requests.

Policy changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date. We will explain material changes to data handling before or when new features become available.
